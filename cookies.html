What this website and the Dropping app store on your device.

In short

  • This website sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser. Because of that, it has no cookie banner: there is nothing to consent to.
  • It loads nothing from other companies. There is no analytics, advertising, social-media or map code.
  • If we ever add something optional, we will list it here and ask first, with Accept and Reject shown equally.

The Dropping app

  • Stores only what it needs to work, in your browser: your session and data, your theme choice, which welcome tours you have seen, and your cookie choice.
  • Shows a consent banner. Maps (OpenStreetMap) stay off until you allow them, and you can change your mind any time with “Cookie settings”.
  • The app has its own full table of what it stores, in its Cookie policy.

Other companies

  • This website: none. The font, logo and every image are served from this site, and the page loads no scripts, trackers, maps, chat tools or social-media buttons from other companies.
  • The Dropping app, only if you allow maps: OpenStreetMap supplies map pictures and receives your IP address and the map area you view.
  • Your Mobile Money provider (MTN, Telecel or AirtelTigo) receives the wallet number and amount when you pay Dropping or redeem earnings. In the pilot this payment step is simulated and no provider is contacted.
