When you get your money back and when a fee applies.

Cancelling before a courier is assigned

Free. If you already paid, the full amount is refunded.

Cancelling after a courier is assigned

A GH₵ 5 cancellation fee applies and the rest is refunded. The fee is shown before you cancel.

After pickup

A booking cannot be cancelled once the parcel is collected. You can ask for a return instead.

Returns

If a parcel cannot be delivered and is returned to you, a return charge applies, 50% of the original fee. It is shown and agreed before the return starts.

If we do not deliver

If we cancel, cannot deliver or lose the parcel, you are not charged for the delivery, or the amount you paid is refunded, as decided in the support case. Compensation for loss or damage follows the claims policy, which is [to be set before launch].

How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the Mobile Money wallet, card or account that paid, and we tell you when one has been sent. How long it takes to appear depends on your provider. Business-invoice orders are credited on your statement.

Where a refund is not due

When the parcel was delivered as booked, when the recipient was unavailable after attempts were made, or when the parcel was prohibited or wrongly described.
