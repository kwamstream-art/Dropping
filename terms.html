The rules for using Dropping Delivery during the Accra pilot.

Who can use Dropping

Dropping is for businesses and adults aged 18 and over. By registering you confirm you are 18 or older and allowed to act for your business or to work as a courier.

What the service is

Same-day parcel delivery in the areas of Accra shown on this website. Delivery times are estimates, not promises. Areas, prices and pickup windows can change, and the price you see before you confirm is the price you pay for that booking.

Food, Rides, Cargo and Moving are ideas we are planning and are not available. These terms cover deliveries only. We will publish terms for any new service before it opens.

Your account

Keep your details accurate and your sign-in private. You are responsible for bookings made from your account. Tell us at once if someone else is using it.

What you can send

Nothing that is prohibited, such as illegal goods, weapons, hazardous goods, live animals and perishable or temperature-controlled items. You confirm each parcel is not prohibited. Couriers and Dropping may refuse, check or return a parcel that looks unsafe, illegal or different from the booking.

Prices and payment

  • You see an itemised price, including every charge, before you confirm.
  • Every payment is made to Dropping, never to the courier. Dropping pays the courier after the delivery is complete.
  • The only later charges are in the Refund policy: a GH₵ 5 cancellation fee after a courier is assigned, and a return charge (50% of the original fee) when a parcel must come back.

Couriers

Couriers apply, are checked before they can take jobs, and may be paid only when a delivery is complete and the business’s payment has been received. A problem report on a delivery holds the payout while we look into it.

Delivery problems

Failed attempts, delays, loss and damage are recorded and handled as support cases. The compensation limit is [to be set before launch].

Our responsibility

We will deliver with reasonable care and skill. To the extent the law allows, we are not responsible for losses we could not reasonably foresee, or caused by wrong addresses, unavailable recipients, prohibited items or events outside our control. Nothing here limits rights you have under Ghanaian law that cannot be limited. [Liability limit to be set by counsel.]

Ending use

You can stop using Dropping at any time. We may suspend an account that breaks these terms or the law.

Disputes and law
