This website

This website collects no personal data on its own. It sets no cookies, stores nothing in your browser and has no analytics. The registration form on the home page opens your own email app with what you typed. Nothing is sent to us until you press send in your email app, and the website does not keep a copy.

What the Dropping service collects, and only what it needs

  • Businesses: business name, contact person, phone, email and pickup addresses, to verify the business, run its account and send service messages.
  • Recipients: name, phone, delivery address and area. A GhanaPost digital address and landmark are optional. Used only to complete and track that delivery.
  • Parcels: category, description, size and handling notes, to price and carry them and to refuse prohibited items.
  • Couriers: at application, name, phone, vehicle type and the areas you can work in. During verification we separately ask for ID, licence and vehicle documents and payout details. Location is collected only while on a job and cleared when the job ends.
  • Payments: the Mobile Money network, wallet number and a payment reference. We never see or store a PIN and never ask for it.
  • We do not collect photos of people, contacts, or data to build advertising profiles.

Why we use it

  • To carry out the delivery you or your business booked (contract).
  • To take and pay out money, keep records, prevent fraud and meet legal duties.
  • To send service messages about a delivery or account. These are not marketing.
  • To send news and offers only if you tick the separate opt-in box. Every email has an unsubscribe link and you can stop at any time.

Who sees it

  • Couriers see the pickup and drop-off area first, and the full address and recipient contact only after accepting the job.
  • A recipient with a tracking link sees status only, and the link expires.
  • Dispatch and administrators see what they need to run deliveries and resolve problems.
  • We do not sell personal data and do not share it for advertising.

Outside companies, listed in full:

  • This website: none. The font, logo and every image are served from this site, and the page loads no scripts, trackers, maps, chat tools or social-media buttons from other companies.
  • The Dropping app, only if you allow maps: OpenStreetMap supplies map pictures and receives your IP address and the map area you view.
  • Your Mobile Money provider (MTN, Telecel or AirtelTigo) receives the wallet number and amount when you pay Dropping or redeem earnings. In the pilot this payment step is simulated and no provider is contacted.

Children

Dropping accounts are for people aged 18 and over, and we do not knowingly collect data from children. If a parcel is for a child under 18, the sender must give a parent or guardian’s name and phone instead of the child’s, confirm that the parent or guardian agrees, and we message that adult only. The courier hands the parcel to the parent or guardian. If you think a child’s data reached us without a parent’s agreement, contact us and we will delete it.

How long we keep it

Recipient details on completed deliveries are removed after 365 days. Courier location is cleared when a job ends. Financial records are kept as long as the law requires. You can ask us to delete your data sooner, unless we must keep it by law.

Your rights

  • See the data we hold about you and get a copy.
  • Have wrong data corrected or deleted.
  • Object to or restrict how we use it, and withdraw consent (for example for marketing) at any time.
  • Complain to the Data Protection Commission of Ghana.
  • To use these rights contact [privacy email to be added] or 030 000 0000 (placeholder).

Email and unsubscribing

Marketing email goes only to people who opted in, and every email carries a one-click unsubscribe link. Emails about a delivery, payment or account are service messages, so they continue while you have an account, and they say why you are getting them.

Security

We limit who can see personal data by role, show phone numbers partly hidden where full numbers are not needed, expire tracking links and keep an audit log of staff actions. No system is perfectly secure. We will tell affected people and the regulator if a breach puts them at risk, as the law requires.

We will update this page and tell you about important changes. Last updated 30 October 2026.
