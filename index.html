    How it works

    1. 1

      Book

      Enter the pickup and drop-off, the parcel details and a pickup time. You see the price first.

    2. 2

      Pay with Mobile Money

      Approve a prompt on your phone: MTN MoMo, Telecel Cash or AirtelTigo Money. Every payment goes to Dropping, never to the courier.

    3. 3

      Track and confirm

      Your recipient gets a tracking link with a live map and confirms when the parcel arrives.

      Questions

      Is Dropping available everywhere in Ghana?

      No. The pilot covers selected areas of Accra only. See the list above.

      How fast is delivery?

      It is a same-day service. Book before the cutoff and pick a pickup window. Times shown are estimates and not guaranteed.

      How do I pay?

      With Mobile Money, a card, a bank transfer, or a business invoice where we have approved it. All payments are made to Dropping.

      What if a parcel is lost or damaged?

      Report it from your order or the tracking link. We open a support case with a named owner. The compensation limit is set out in our claims policy.

      What data do you collect?

      Only what a delivery needs. This website uses no cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. See the Privacy policy.
